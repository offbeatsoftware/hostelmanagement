1. Getting started

Start the program from the desktop shortcut Hostel Management System.

  1. Enter the user name and password. A new installation uses admin / admin; change the password at once (section 12).
  2. Choose the hostel to work on and click Sign in.

The menu on the left opens each screen. The hostel you work on is shown at the top right; change it there at any time. Colleges, rooms, students, invoices, payments, fee reminders, reports and the dashboard always show the selected hostel only.

Fields marked with * are required. Messages in red under a form explain what to correct; nothing is saved until the form is correct.

2. Setting up a hostel

  1. Hostels: click Add and enter the hostel name (address, phone and email are optional). Each new hostel gets the sharing types Single, Double and Triple.
  2. Colleges: add the colleges whose students stay in the hostel.
  3. Rooms: add the rooms: room number, floor, sharing type and whether the room is for boys or girls. The sharing type only decides how many students fit in the room (Single 1, Double 2, Triple 3). There is no rent per room: the fee is agreed with each student at check-in (section 4).

3. Students, father and mother, photos and I-cards

Add a student

  1. Students > Add.
  2. Student tab: name, mobile and admission date are required; choose the gender too, it decides the rooms the student can be given. The Aadhaar number is checked for typing mistakes.
  3. College & Stay tab: college, course and class.
  4. Father & Mother tab: the father's name and mobile are required; his email and the mother's name, mobile and email are optional.
    • Invoices, receipts and fee reminders go to the father's email (the mother's when the father has none).
    • Attendance emails go to the mother's email (the father's when the mother has none).
  5. Photo & Aadhaar tab: click Take Photo to use the computer's webcam (the picture is live; click Capture, then Use Photo, or Retake), or Choose Photo to pick a JPG or PNG file. Add the Aadhaar card (image or PDF). Each file can be at most 5 MB.

Use the search box (student, father or mother name or mobile) and the filters (college, course, class, Active/Left) to find students. The Aadhaar number is shown masked (XXXX XXXX 1234) in lists and reports.

If Take Photo says Windows does not allow the camera: open Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Camera and switch on Camera access and Let desktop apps access your camera.

I-cards

Select one or more students (hold Ctrl or Shift to select several) and click:

  • I-Card: the student identity card with photo, name, father, college, room and bed, mobile numbers and valid till 30 June (end of the academic year).
  • Transport I-Card: the transport pass with photo, only for students whose fee for this academic year includes transport.

The cards are wallet size (85.6 x 54 mm), eight on an A4 page. Choose where to save the PDF; it opens for printing. Students without a photo get an empty photo box, and the program tells you which ones.

4. Rooms, check-in and the residency agreement

Check-in and the student's fee

  1. Room Allocation > Check-in.
  2. Choose the student, the room (only rooms for the student's gender with a free bed are listed) and the bed. The lowest free bed is chosen for you; change it if needed.
  3. Enter the room rent for the year and the transport for the year agreed with this student (leave transport empty if the student does not use it). Students in the same room can have different amounts. The total is shown below.
  4. Click Check-in. The program creates the student's invoice for the academic year of the check-in date (July to June) with the room rent and transport. The full amount is due even when the student joins during the year.
  5. After check-in the program offers to create the residency agreement PDF straight away.

Transfer and check-out

Select the student in Room Allocation and click Transfer (to another room, same day) or Check-out. Check-out sets the student to Left; the history of every room is kept. Neither changes the student's fee: use Edit Fee on the Invoices screen if the amount should change. A student who has left still owes the pending amount.

Residency agreement

Students > select the student > Agreement PDF. Choose where to save it; it opens for printing. The agreement fills in the student's name, S/o or D/o with the father's name, address, Boys or Girls hostel, room and bed number, the number of beds in the room, the check-in date and the annual fee (the room rent plus transport agreed for the year, in figures and words). Students without an address get a dotted line to fill in by hand.

To change the wording, click Agreement Text on the Students screen. Words in curly brackets, such as {StudentName} or {BedNumber}, are filled in for each student. Restore Default brings back the original text.

5. Night attendance

Attendance (under Students) lists the students who are in a room of the hostel that night, with room and bed. Everyone starts as Present.

  1. At night, open Attendance; today's date is shown.
  2. Untick Present for each student who is not in the hostel (add a remark if useful) and click Save Attendance.
  3. The saved absent students appear on the right. Click Email Parents to email the mother of each absent student (the father when the mother has no email), with a copy to the admin. Each parent is emailed once per date; the program asks before emailing again.

Choose another date to see or correct an earlier night. The text of the email is on Settings > Email Settings > Attendance email.

6. Fees and invoices

Each student has one invoice per academic year (July to June) with the room rent and the transport agreed for that year. It is created at check-in. Invoice numbers look like SBH/2026-27/0001.

  • Edit Fee: change the room rent or transport of the selected invoice (for example when a student stops using transport). The new total cannot be less than what has already been paid.
  • New Year Fees: at the start of a new academic year, choose the year. The list shows every student still in a room who has no fee for that year, with last year's amounts for reference. Type each student's new room rent and transport and click Create Fees; students left empty are skipped.
  • View / Print opens the invoice PDF: room rent, transport, total fee, every payment received (date, receipt, method, reference, amount), total paid and the amount pending. Save PDF saves it elsewhere, and Email sends it to the father (or the mother).
  • Filters: search, status (Unpaid, Partly paid, Paid) and academic year. An invoice can be deleted only while it has no payments and no emails.

Example: room rent 50,000 + transport 12,000 = total 62,000. The student pays 10,000 at admission and 5,000 after 15 days: paid 15,000, pending 47,000.

7. Payments and receipts

  1. Invoices > select the invoice > Record Payment (or Payments > Record, or Fee Reminders > Record Payment).
  2. The pending amount is filled in; change it to the amount actually paid. The student can pay any amount, any number of times, but never more than the pending amount.
  3. Choose Cash, UPI, Bank transfer or Cheque. For everything except cash, enter the UPI transaction id, bank reference or cheque number.

Each payment gets a receipt number such as SBH/R/2026-27/0001. On the Payments screen, View / Print opens the receipt (with the amount in words and the balance still pending) and Email Receipt sends it to the father (or the mother). A payment entered by mistake can be deleted; its amount becomes pending again.

8. Fee reminders

Fee Reminders (under Billing) lists every student who still owes money, including students who have left, with the total fee, paid, pending, who the email goes to and when the last reminder was sent. There are no due dates: send a reminder whenever you want. Reminders are never sent automatically.

  1. Tick the Send box of each student to remind (Select All ticks everyone who has an email; Clear unticks all).
  2. On the right is the ready-made reminder text. Change it if you like; words in curly brackets such as {Pending} are filled in for each student. Preview shows exactly what the selected student's parent will receive.
  3. Click Send Reminder. Each email goes to the father (or the mother when the father has no email), with the invoice PDF and a copy to the admin. Students without any parent email are listed afterwards.

Save as Default keeps a changed text for next time; Saved Text brings back the saved one. Export PDF saves the list of pending fees.

9. Email (Gmail)

All emails are sent from the hostel's Gmail account shribalajihostelsuddhowala@gmail.com, and a copy goes to the admin's email (section 12). Gmail needs a 16 letter app password, not the normal password.

Create the app password (once)

  1. In a browser, sign in to the hostel's Gmail account and open myaccount.google.com > Security.
  2. Turn on 2-Step Verification (Google sends a code to the mobile number).
  3. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords, type a name such as Hostel App and click Create.
  4. Copy the 16 letters shown in the yellow box (Google shows them only once).

Enter it in the program

  1. Settings > Email Settings > Gmail account tab: paste the app password and click Save.
  2. Type an address in Send test to and click Send Test Email.

The tabs Invoice email, Receipt email, Fee reminder email and Attendance email hold the texts of the emails; words in curly brackets are filled in. Email history lists every email, sent or failed, with the reason for a failure.

If the Gmail password is ever changed, Google cancels the app password: create a new one and enter it again. The app password is also needed again after moving to another PC.

11. Backup and restore

A backup is one zip file with the database and all photos and Aadhaar cards, saved in C:\HostelManagement\Backups.

  • An automatic backup is made once a day when the program is closed; the last 30 are kept.
  • Backup Now makes a backup at any time; these are never deleted.
  • Second copy in: choose a USB drive or a Google Drive / OneDrive folder; every backup is copied there too, so it survives if the PC fails.

Restore: select a backup and click Restore Selected... (or Restore From File...). All current data is replaced by the backup; the program first saves a backup of the current data (so a restore can be undone) and then restarts.

12. Admin account

Settings > Admin Account: enter the admin's email (it receives a copy of every email sent to a father or mother) and phone, and change the password (at least 6 characters; enter the current password first).

13. Rules the program follows

TopicRule
Academic yearJuly to June; shown as 2026-27.
FeeAgreed per student at check-in: room rent and transport for the year. One invoice per student per academic year; the full amount is due even when joining mid-year. A new fee every year (New Year Fees).
RoomsBoys or girls; the sharing type decides the number of beds only; one student per bed. Transfer and check-out on any day; they do not change the fee.
PaymentsAny amount, any number of times, never more than pending. Balance = total fee minus everything paid. Reference required except for cash. No GST, deposit, discounts or late fee.
RemindersNo due dates; the admin sends fee reminders whenever he wants, never automatically.
EmailsSent only when you click a button. Fees: father, else mother. Attendance: mother, else father. Copy to the admin.
I-cardsValid till 30 June of the academic year. Transport I-card only with transport in this year's fee.
