Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shri Balaji Hostel, near B.F.I.T., Suddhowala, Dehradun, Uttarakhand 248007, India ("we", "us", "the hostel") collects, uses, stores and protects the personal information of students who stay at the hostel and of their parents or guardians. It applies to information we collect at admission, during the stay, through this website, and through the messages we send by email and WhatsApp.

1. Information we collect

  • Student details: name, photograph, date of birth, college and course, mobile number, email address, permanent address, identity document (such as Aadhaar), room and bed allotted, check in and check out dates.
  • Parent or guardian details: names, mobile numbers (including WhatsApp numbers) and email addresses.
  • Fee and attendance records: invoices, payments, receipts, pending amounts and night attendance.
  • Messages: the content, date and delivery status of emails and WhatsApp messages we send, and any replies you send to us.

This website does not use cookies, does not run analytics or advertising trackers, and does not collect any personal information from visitors.

2. How we use the information

  • To admit students, allot rooms and prepare the residency agreement and identity card.
  • To issue invoices and receipts and to keep fee accounts.
  • To send parents fee reminders and attendance alerts (for example when a student is absent at night attendance) by email and through the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta.
  • To contact parents in an emergency and to meet legal obligations.

We do not use the information for marketing, and we never sell, rent or trade it.

3. WhatsApp messages

We send WhatsApp messages only to the mobile numbers that parents or students give us at admission, and only about the student's stay: fees, payments and attendance. These messages are sent through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API). When a message is sent, the recipient's phone number and the message content are processed by Meta Platforms, Inc. and WhatsApp LLC under their own terms and privacy policy (whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy).

You can stop WhatsApp messages at any time by replying STOP to our message, or by writing to shribalajihostelsuddhowala@gmail.com. We will then contact you by email or telephone only.

4. Sharing of information

We share personal information only:

  • with service providers who deliver our messages (Google Gmail for email and Meta for WhatsApp), only as needed to deliver them;
  • with the student's college or the authorities when required by law or for the safety of the student;
  • with the student's parents or guardians, about the student's stay.

5. Storage and security

Records are kept on the hostel office computer in a password protected hostel management application. Passwords and access keys for email and WhatsApp are stored encrypted. Backups are kept securely by the management. Access is limited to the hostel administrator.

6. How long we keep information

We keep student and fee records for as long as the student stays at the hostel and afterwards for as long as required for accounts, tax and legal purposes. Message history is kept with the fee and attendance records.

7. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian law, you may ask us to see, correct or update your information, to withdraw your consent to WhatsApp messages, or to delete information we no longer need to keep. See our Data Deletion page or write to us.

8. Children

Where a student is under 18, we collect the student's information with the consent of a parent or guardian, who signs the residency agreement.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

10. Contact
