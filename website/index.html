About the hostel

Shri Balaji Hostel provides residential accommodation to students of colleges in and around Sudhowala, Dehradun. The hostel is run by its owner, Sh. Samar Makhija, and is located close to B.F.I.T. (Baba Farid Institute of Technology).

Each student is allotted a bed in a furnished shared room with an attached bathroom, under an 11 month residency agreement signed with the student and parent. The management keeps parents informed about fees and attendance by email and WhatsApp, so families always know their child is safe and the account is up to date.

Facilities

Meals included

Vegetarian meals prepared in the hostel kitchen. The menu is decided by the management with the students every quarter.

Wi-Fi

Internet access for study and online classes, included in the fee.

1500 litre solar geyser supply, plus a 10000 litre normal water supply.

Inverter and generator backup; the generator is used during examinations.

Furnished rooms

Shared rooms with separate beds, furniture and an attached bathroom.

Electricity and water

Electricity and water charges are part of the annual fee.

Safe and disciplined

8:00 PM return time, parents informed of absences, no ragging, no alcohol or tobacco.

Updates for parents

Fee reminders

Parents receive the invoice, receipts and reminders of any pending fee by email and on WhatsApp.

Attendance alerts

If a student is absent at night attendance, a parent is informed by email and on WhatsApp the same day.

Your choice

Parents can ask us at any time to stop WhatsApp messages by replying STOP or writing to us.

Key hostel rules

  • Students must return to the hostel by 8:00 PM; parents must call the management if a student will be late.
  • No night stay outside the hostel without a written request and telephone confirmation from parents.
  • Ragging is strictly prohibited.
  • Tobacco, alcohol and intoxicants are not allowed in the hostel premises.
  • Heaters, immersion rods, irons and similar appliances are not allowed in rooms.
  • Outsiders are not allowed to stay overnight.
  • Meals are served in the dining area, not inside rooms.
  • Loud music and loudspeakers are not permitted.
  • All disputes are subject to Dehradun court jurisdiction.

The complete rules are part of the residency agreement given at admission.
