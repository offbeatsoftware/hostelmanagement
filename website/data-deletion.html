Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 10 November 2026

You can ask Shri Balaji Hostel to delete your personal information or your WhatsApp number from our records at any time.

To stop WhatsApp messages

Reply STOP to any WhatsApp message from the hostel. We will stop sending WhatsApp messages to that number.

To delete your data

  1. Send an email to shribalajihostelsuddhowala@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request.
  2. Write the student's name, the room number (if known) and the mobile number or email address you want removed.
  3. We will confirm your request by email or phone and complete it within 30 days.

Some records, such as invoices, receipts and the signed residency agreement, must be kept for accounting and legal purposes. These are retained only for as long as the law requires and are not used for messaging. We will tell you if any record cannot be deleted and why.

You may also deliver a written request at the hostel office: Shri Balaji Hostel, near B.F.I.T., Sudhowala, Dehradun, Uttarakhand, India.
